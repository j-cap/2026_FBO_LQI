Does fleet cooperation have room to help?

Stage A · corrected saved evidence · no new simulation or optimization

These curves show how acceptance changes with the requirement. They do not select a justified engineering threshold or demonstrate an experiment saving.

What the evidence covers

7,680 grid evaluations; 530 unique donor-transfer evaluations; 42,400 transfer episodes; 72 source calibration runs. Repeated donor labels are not independent evidence.

All tested target conditions pass the per-maneuver threshold for a source run once it exceeds that run's worst maneuver mean. Across the saved source runs these thresholds range from 0.9040 to 0.9445 deg/s. This concerns five other vehicles at five bandwidths, not a complete donor-inclusive fleet test.

At the same numeric threshold applied to the different, combined-maneuver grid score, 23.4%, 23.8% of the sampled weights pass all 15 vehicle/bandwidth conditions within the respective realizations. These are screening results; they cannot substitute for per-maneuver acceptance. Broad shared regions remain plausible.

The payload vehicle at tau=0.4 s has the largest grid cost for 192/256 candidates in one realization and 153/256 in the other. This suggests a demanding-condition calibration baseline worth testing before a sophisticated allocation algorithm. It does not prove that this vehicle is always sufficient.

Numerical evidence

Transfer episodes: 0 physical failures and 0 unresolved checks. Grid: 525/7680 candidates have a saved passing validation integration check; that different-bank check does not certify every grid score.

Interpretation and next decision

A broad region at an acceptable engineering threshold favors simple transfer. A narrow region with vehicle-dependent exclusions motivates further study, but these descriptive curves cannot establish that cooperation saves experiments. Obtain the engineering requirement before Stage B; do not choose a threshold because it makes transfer fail.

Coverage limits

  • Grid costs combine maneuvers; no grid true-state scores.
  • Most grid candidates have no saved coarse/fine validation check.
  • No common grid points across the two fleet realizations.
  • Direct transfers cover five OTHER vehicles; donor at all bandwidths was not tested.
  • Bandwidth variants and optimizer repetitions are not independent fleets.
  • Threshold curves are retrospective descriptions, not a policy or sample-efficiency experiment.
