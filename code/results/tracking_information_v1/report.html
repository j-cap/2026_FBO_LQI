Which historical information helps calibration?

A paired 2×2 ablation of two additional historical information channels: the objective GP prior mean and the feasibility prior used by acquisition. All four BO arms share the same catalogue, six initial target observations, target-learning rules, hyperparameter policy, budgets, source histories, and measurement-noise realization.

00: no additional historical prior. 10: cost mean only. 01: feasibility only. 11: both. Target objective and target feasibility learning remain active in every arm. The fixed catalogue is a separate six-query benchmark.

Interpretation

The catalogue itself still uses historical costs and feasibility, and the historical cost GP was fitted to eligible source objectives. This test identifies the usefulness of the two algorithmic channels after a common initialization, not the value of raw cost data without any historical eligibility information. The cost channel includes both the overall historical mean level and its gain-dependent variation.

The full source archive, nine deterministic vehicles, two target-noise realizations, and 71-candidate pool remain fixed. Source-history sparsity, new physical rollouts and new reference shapes are not tested here. Twenty optimizer seeds are not twenty independent fleets. See docs/tracking_information.md for the frozen attribution protocol.
