Gain-domain audit: sampling density and genuine boundary extension

This offline audit asks whether the original 71-point controller pool was too coarse or its physical upper bounds too restrictive. The nominal controller, plant, trajectories, objective and eligibility rules remain fixed.

Original: the frozen 71 gain vectors. Dense original: the nested 1,024 Sobol draws plus original scaling anchors and two old-upper-face probes (reduced Sobol counts in smoke). Expanded: the entire dense-original set plus only points outside the old box, sampled in the 0.1–30 multiplier box and supplemented by fixed boundary profiles and common scalings. Expanded Sobol points still inside the old box are discarded. Thus an old-boundary or interior-resolution improvement cannot be counted as evidence of a boundary extension.

Interpretation

No new online BO requests, kernel training or public spectral-basis construction are performed. The old normalized gain transform is retained, so outside points have coordinates above 1. All failed and unresolved offline attempts remain in the availability counts. The original online studies and their reference labels are preserved. This audit does not rerank their recorded policies under a new benchmark.

Upper limits are extended for all three gains; lower limits remain unchanged. A selected point near an expanded boundary still leaves that direction unresolved. Future larger-domain online BO would need a scalable feature representation and a separately fixed protocol.
