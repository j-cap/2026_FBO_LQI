Representation and audit scope

Public grid: 1,278 inputs; feature width: 257. Relative kernel Frobenius error 0.521%; maximum relative diagonal error 5.112%. The earlier targets are 2% and 5%. Rank 256 is retained; its slight diagonal-target miss is reported, not tuned away.

Only scheduled-context predictions are evaluated; the Gaussian messages, evidence mixture and total variance are unchanged. Reference communication counts the same per-component float64 matrices/vectors and per-recipient broadcasts. Public-basis distribution (23,648,112 bytes per method/fleet if distributed to every client) is separate. This is not a communication experiment. Unused end-of-final-round training is excluded from decision snapshots.

All paid choices are audited before loading evaluator outcomes. A/B audits restore logged pre-query GP parameters and refit counters; they do not claim identical fresh optimization across numerical platforms. D/E predictions and choices are reconstructed from their own earlier histories. Online failures and unresolved attempts remain charged.

Dense calibration and held-out validation are fresh evaluator-only work. All methods share a held-out bank for the common physical plant, conditional on the stated programme/noise replication scope. Source hashes, pinned basis, programmes, online raw simulator records and evaluator batches accompany this report.
