Six-speed fixed small-bonus pilot

Question: does one fixed, restrained cost uncertainty bonus recover useful discoveries without losing the zero-bonus shared model's failure profile? A/B retain beta=2; D/E use beta=0 or beta=0.5 in every context. No frequency-specific rule, coefficient sweep or adaptive retuning is used.

Six speeds, three frequencies, nine homogeneous clients, 24 paid requests each (three clients/eight requests in smoke). The factory test, exact-context peer-first rule, local eligibility penalty, kernel, Bayesian messages and priors are fixed. Histories and peer catalogues are separate per arm. Failed tests remain charged.

Exploratory evidence from three programme/noise seeds on one plant. Primary success first averages encountered contexts within each client, then clients and seeds equally. Unvisited contexts are separate. Missing or ineligible recommendations remain failures. No confidence interval or confirmation claim.
