Six-speed matched zero-bonus pilot

Goal: beat A, then B, and measure whether sharing the contextual cost model adds value when D256 and E256 both omit the cost uncertainty bonus. Fresh programme/noise seeds; cold histories; six discrete speeds 20–30 m/s; three frequencies; one externally assigned speed/frequency context per trial.

Matched methods

method bonus cost_information
A 2 Local adaptive GP
B 2 Local RFF mean + residual GP
D256 (beta=2) 2 Shared/local spectral Gaussian model
E256 (beta=2) 2 Local spectral Gaussian model
D256 (beta=0) 0 Shared/local spectral Gaussian model
E256 (beta=0) 0 Local spectral Gaussian model

All methods use the same paid exact-context peer-first rule, one charged factory test, a 24-test local cap (8 in smoke), and distinct untested gain/context pairs. Peer catalogues and histories evolve independently per method. The zero-bonus score is predicted log cost plus the unchanged negative-log eligibility penalty: mean(log J) - lambda * log(max(p_eligible, floor)). The beta=2 controls additionally subtract 2 * sqrt(latent variance). Full Bayesian covariance, evidence mixture and communication accounting are retained.

Exploratory pilot. Three seeds on one homogeneous compact plant. Primary success averages encountered contexts within each vehicle, then vehicles and seeds equally. Failed or missing recommendations remain failures; unvisited contexts are separate. A success is a held-out cost within 5% of the independent calibration-selected reference. No confidence interval or confirmation claim.
