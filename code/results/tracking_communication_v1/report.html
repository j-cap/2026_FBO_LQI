Compact communication: matched 1/2/4-round pilot

Question: how much synchronization can be removed while retaining the benefit of shared cost information over A, B and matched independent E?

9 homogeneous clients, 24 paid requests each, 1,292 public gains and 18 speed/frequency contexts. Cold histories, one paid factory test, the same charged peer-first rule and the same local eligibility penalty. A/B beta=2; D/E beta=0.5. The dynamics, trajectories, objective, prior and controller bounds remain those of the frozen dense-gain reference.

All four arms refresh their own observations every round. Both the peer-gain catalogue and D cost records synchronize only after every k rounds. A decision in round r can use remote information through k·floor((r−1)/k), plus its own current data. The coordinator relays summaries; recipients reconstruct the exact shared/local GP. Failed labels remain private. Public input IDs and sender/trial IDs retain observation provenance.
