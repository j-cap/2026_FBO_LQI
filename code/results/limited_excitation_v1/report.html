Limited-excitation simulation study — Gate 1

Research assumptions informed by vehicle-test standard scopes; no ISO compliance or industrial commissioning claim.

Question: do limited observations leave decision-relevant uncertainty? All variants have identical small-slip stiffness, mass, geometry and actuator. Only effective tire saturation coefficient varies. LQI synthesis is given the exact common tangent model and actuator: an optimistic isolation of nonlinear uncertainty, not an identification result.

1. Does the choice of weights matter?

Full-state, noise-free closed-loop evaluation is an offline diagnostic. Same candidate pool and gains across variants. Reference is best sampled feasible weight, not a global optimum.

2. Is the uncertainty visible in permitted measurements?

Observation channels: yaw rate, actual lateral acceleration and actual wheel angle. Stationary Gaussian AR(1) measurement noise is stipulated, with known marginal standard deviations and correlation. Pairwise error is Phi(-D/2), where D is the whitened distance between exact mean trajectories, under equal priors and two known plant hypotheses. This is an optimistic local diagnostic with known nuisance parameters. It is not the error of a fitted estimator, a three-class error, or a sensor guarantee. Near-zero error means low excitation alone does not hide these variants under this noise model. Large error does not imply different weights are required.

Actual ay/angle/rate limits are monitored at integration substeps; aborted tests are excluded from the separation calculation and reported. Input amplitudes are determined from the common tangent model, never scaled using target nonlinear outcomes. The cap is a permission envelope, not a demand to reach its boundary.

Primary requests

Three fixed tracking fractions (10%, 20%, 30% of reference peak). Acceptable weights satisfy physical/tracking constraints and lie within 10% of each plant's best sampled steering RMS. These are inherited-style developmental choices, not external requirements. The full 1–30% grid is descriptive only; do not select a favorable threshold after inspection.

Interpretation and stopping rule

  • Large acceptable-set overlap: common weights remain a strong answer, even with nonlinear mismatch.
  • Different choices but easily distinguished plants: allow a strong local estimator before claiming fleet value.
  • Different choices and poorly distinguished plants: a candidate information gap; still needs a practical restriction and an observable source–target relationship.
  • No sampled feasible solution: distinguish controller/task/candidate-pool limitations from transfer failure. Empty intersections in a small pool are not proof of disjoint continuous sets.

The hindsight common controller and donor-specific best weights use evaluation truth and are diagnostics, not deployable baselines. No fleet learner, source budget, trained local estimator, or calibrated reliability bound is implemented in Gate 1. No new noise enters controller feedback; the observation-noise model applies only to the characterization information diagnostic.

Sources and protocol

ISO scopes: 15037-1 (general conditions), 7401 (transient response), 4138 (steady-state response). The multisine, amplitudes, noise, duration and requirements here are our own research choices. See docs/limited_excitation.md and manifest.json.
